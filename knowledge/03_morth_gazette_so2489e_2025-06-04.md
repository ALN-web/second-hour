# About this summary

This file summarises the cashless treatment guidelines notified by the Ministry of Road Transport and Highways as Gazette S.O. 2489(E) on 4 June 2025. Second Hour could not verify the full gazette text during this build. Only points that are also confirmed in the PIB release of 11 March 2026 are listed here. Sections not listed were NOT VERIFIED and should not be relied upon.

# Verified points (cross-checked with PIB 11 March 2026)

Cashless treatment is up to ₹1.5 lakh per victim for a maximum of 7 days from the date of the accident, at designated hospitals. The hospital initiates police authentication; police respond within 24 hours, or 48 hours for life-threatening cases. Hospitals are reimbursed from the Motor Vehicle Accident Fund.

# Not verified

Detailed procedures for grievance redressal, state-level implementation and non-designated hospitals were not verified by Second Hour. Ask the hospital desk or the State Health Agency for these details.

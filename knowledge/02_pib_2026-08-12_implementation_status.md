# Implementation status as of 31 July 2026

As of 31 July 2026, 22,481 road accident victims across 34 States and Union Territories were covered under PM-RAHAT, and 18,149 cases were approved.

# Police response statistics

3,674 victims were discharged after no police response was received within the prescribed timelines.

# Eligibility statistics

658 life-threatening cases were not found eligible.

# Hospital claims

Hospital claims worth ₹12.59 crore were approved, of which ₹10.56 crore had been paid to hospitals.

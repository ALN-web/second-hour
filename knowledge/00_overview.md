# What is PM-RAHAT

PM-RAHAT is the Government of India scheme for cashless treatment of road accident victims. It provides cashless treatment up to ₹1.5 lakh per victim, for a maximum of 7 days from the date of the accident, at designated hospitals.

# Which hospitals

Designated hospitals are empanelled AB PM-JAY hospitals that meet National Health Authority (NHA) guidelines. Anyone can dial 112 to find the nearest designated hospital and request an ambulance.

# What the family should know

The cashless amount is paid to the hospital, not to the family. Hospitals are reimbursed from the Motor Vehicle Accident Fund within 10 days of State Health Agency approval. Police authentication is started by the hospital, not the family. Second Hour is not an emergency service: in an emergency, call 112.

# Cashless treatment cover

Road accident victims are eligible for cashless treatment up to ₹1.5 lakh per victim, for a maximum period of 7 days from the date of the accident, at designated hospitals.

# Designated hospitals

Designated hospitals are empanelled AB PM-JAY hospitals that meet National Health Authority (NHA) guidelines.

# Stabilisation treatment

Stabilisation treatment is available for up to 24 hours in non-life-threatening cases, or up to 48 hours in life-threatening cases, at designated hospitals, subject to police response.

# Police authentication

The hospital starts police authentication on the TMS platform and pushes the treatment ID to the district police through eDAR. Police have up to 24 hours to respond, or up to 48 hours in life-threatening cases. These timelines apply to the hospital and the police; they are not deadlines for the victim's family.

# Payment to hospitals

Hospitals are reimbursed from the Motor Vehicle Accident Fund within 10 days of approval by the State Health Agency. The money goes to the hospital; it is not cash paid to the family.

# Emergency help

Anyone can dial 112 to find the nearest designated hospital and request an ambulance.
